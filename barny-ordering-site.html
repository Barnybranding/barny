How to Order from Barny

From finding the right branding solution to tracking production, ordering is straightforward. Follow these three simple steps.

1

Open our website

Visit the Barny Branding Co. website on your phone, tablet or computer. The homepage gives you quick access to product categories, popular branding solutions, ordering support and the catalogue search. If you already know what you need, use the search bar to find it immediately.

2

Browse and add to cart

Navigate through the product shelves or choose a category that matches your organisation or project. Open any product to review it, then select Add to quote cart. You may combine several products or services and adjust the required quantities before submitting your request.

3

Confirm your order

Open your quote cart, review the selected items and choose Request quote for all. Enter your contact, organisation and delivery details, then submit. Our team will confirm specifications, artwork, quantity, price, payment and production timeline with you.

Track your order

After confirmation, production begins. Barny Branding Co. will provide progress updates through your supplied phone number, email or WhatsApp—from artwork approval and production to dispatch or collection. Keep your order details available whenever you contact us for an update.
